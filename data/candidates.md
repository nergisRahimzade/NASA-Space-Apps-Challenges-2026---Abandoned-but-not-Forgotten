## Date and object notes

- Dates identify the event in each row. Landing dates use UTC; the Apollo 15 entry records first rover deployment/use instead. These dates are not all mission-end or abandonment dates.
- U.S. local-date accounts sometimes put Surveyor 1 and Surveyor 3 a day earlier. Spirit and Opportunity pages also show January 3 and January 24 in Pacific time; the table uses January 4 and January 25 UTC, as explicitly stated in NASA's linked rover resource.
- Eagle and Intrepid refer only to the descent stages left at the landing sites, not the complete two-stage lunar modules.
- Surveyor 3 means the remaining lander structure. Apollo 12 returned its camera, scoop and other parts to Earth.
- Sojourner's date is arrival aboard Pathfinder. It is not the date the rover drove down the deployment ramp.
- Mission-end declarations, last contact and the time hardware stopped working are different events. The table does not treat them as interchangeable or infer exact power-off dates.
