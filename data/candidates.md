## How to fill this table

- **object:** The specific spacecraft, lander, rover, instrument, or other object being considered.
- **body:** Moon or Mars.
- **date:** A verified date in YYYY-MM-DD format. State the event, such as launch, landing, or end of mission, so different dates are not confused. Mark unknown or unverified information as TBD.
- **source link:** A direct NASA or NSSDCA page supporting the entry. The bookmarks above are research starting points, not evidence for individual objects.

Day 1 provides the research bookmarks and blank template. Day 2 adds 10-12 candidates and one sentence explaining why each matters. This template contains no researched candidate records yet.

Keep data/candidates.md tracked in Git; do not ignore the entire data/ directory in this project.
